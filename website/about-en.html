Scanix QR

Version 1.0.3.6

Scanix QR is an Android app for scanning QR codes and barcodes and creating new QR codes. It lets you scan codes using your camera or images stored on your device, and work with the results directly within the app.

App Features

  • Scan QR codes and barcodes using your phone's camera.
  • Use the flashlight while scanning when needed and supported.
  • Read codes from images you select on your device.
  • View, copy, and share scan results.
  • Check links before opening them and display warnings for some suspicious links.
  • Create QR codes for text, links, contact details, and Wi-Fi networks.
  • Preview created QR codes and save or share their images.
  • Save scan results in local history, search the history, and delete results.
  • Customize the appearance and choose Arabic or English in the app settings.

Privacy

The app is designed to process codes on your device and store scan history locally when saving is enabled. An account is not required. When you copy or share results, or open a link, information may be transferred to the app or website you choose.

For more details about permissions, storage, and internet connectivity, please review the Privacy Policy.
