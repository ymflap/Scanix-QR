Scanix QR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Scanix QR handles information when you use its QR code and barcode scanning features. It describes the permissions used, how scan results are stored, and when information may be transferred to other parties.

2. Camera and Images

The app requests camera permission when you use live scanning. You can also select an image from your device to read a code contained in it. The camera and selected image are used to perform the code-reading operation.

Granting camera permission does not mean that the app requires you to create an account. You can manage camera permission through Android settings.

3. Scan Results and Local Storage

The app can save scan results in a local history within the app's storage on your device. Saving depends on the history setting, and you can review and delete saved results inside the app.

Under the current storage implementation, the history keeps up to 200 results. Using the app does not require a user account.

Previously saved results may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android's behavior.

4. Internet Connection and Updates

The app uses an internet connection to check for available update information.

When an update-check request is made, the hosting service may receive ordinary connection data needed to process the request and return a response, in accordance with its own policies.

The internet connection used to check for updates does not, by itself, mean that scan history is sent to a server. Based on the code paths reviewed, no dedicated server for receiving scan results was identified.

5. Copying, Sharing, and Opening Links

You can copy scan results, share them, or open links contained in them. When you choose one of these actions, information may be transferred to another app or website, depending on your choice.

Information you provide to other apps and websites is subject to the privacy policies and terms of the parties operating them. We recommend checking a link's destination before opening it and avoiding sharing sensitive information with untrusted parties.

6. Third-Party Services and Technical Data

The app uses libraries to process QR codes and barcodes and operates within Android. Technical or diagnostic data collected by the operating system or any third-party service used may be subject to that service provider's policies.

We do not claim that all system components and third-party services refrain from collecting technical data; their behavior and policies are independent.

7. Information Security

History results are stored in the app's private storage area. We recommend keeping your device secure, using a screen lock, and deleting results you no longer need.

No electronic method can guarantee absolute security in all circumstances.

8. Children's Privacy

Scanix QR is a code-scanning tool and was not designed to collect children's personal information through an app-specific account system.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or information handling practices change. We will update the last-updated date when appropriate changes are made to this page.
